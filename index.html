How the model works

01 / DATA FIRST

Uses 2026–27 completed regulation games. ESPN's unofficial scoreboard and summary endpoints are used to collect results and box-score totals. Fails closed when data is unavailable.

02 / WAIT FOR SAMPLE

Preview forecasts require one valid current-season game per team. Official WATCH signals require 10 valid games per team. All efficiency is shrunk toward the current-season league average.

03 / FORECAST THE TOTAL

Estimated possessions × combined matchup-adjusted offensive efficiencies / 100. No home-court, injury, or player rotation adjustments yet.

04 / COMPARE TO MARKET

Available US bookmaker totals are compared against the independent projection. A 5.5-point gap creates a research watch, not an actual bet recommendation.

05 / PAPER GRADE

Pregame market snapshots are saved during calibration. Preview forecasts are not saved as paper selections. Once official WATCH signals unlock, potential selections are saved before tipoff and graded against recorded lines and odds. Realized unit returns are tracked independently of any wagers.

06 / KNOW THE LIMITS

This baseline has not been backtested, validated, or calibrated. Market lines can move. The free injury feed is not connected, and public third-party feeds may fail.
