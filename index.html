How the model works

01 / DATA FIRST

Uses 2026–27 completed regulation games. ESPN's unofficial scoreboard and summary endpoints are used to collect results and box-score totals. Fails closed when data is unavailable.

02 / WAIT FOR SAMPLE

Preview forecasts require one valid current-season game per team. Official WATCH signals require 10 valid games per team. V1 uses the exact original Excel matchup arithmetic with raw current-season inputs; V2 additionally shrinks efficiencies toward the current-season league average.

03 / FORECAST THE TOTAL

V1: mean pace × [average(away ORtg, home DRtg) + average(home ORtg, away DRtg)] / 100. V2: league-adjusted, shrinkage-weighted opponent efficiencies. No home-court, injury, or player rotation adjustments yet.

04 / COMPARE TO MARKET

Available US bookmaker totals are compared against the independent projection. A 5.5-point gap creates a research watch, not an actual bet recommendation.

05 / PAPER GRADE

Pregame market snapshots are saved during calibration. Preview forecasts are not graded as paper selections. Once official WATCH signals unlock, V1 and V2 selections are saved independently before tipoff and graded against captured odds. Realized unit returns are tracked independently of any wagers.

06 / KNOW THE LIMITS

The current-season implementations have not been backtested, validated, or calibrated. The original workbook's 40–28 record is a recorded result, not evidence of a bias-free pregame backtest. Market lines can move. The free injury feed is not connected, and public third-party feeds may fail.
